# Recipes

Worked examples, one musical outcome each. Every recipe names the pads and encoders
exactly, says what you should hear, and links to the page that explains the machinery.

If a recipe assumes a page you have never opened, read the first two sections of
[Start here](../start-here.md) first — it takes five minutes and makes these readable.

## How a recipe is written

Goal · what to set up first · numbered steps · what you should hear · variations · links.
If a step depends on a page being closed or a radio being armed, the recipe says so —
those are the two things that most often make a recipe "not work".
