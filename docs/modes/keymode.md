# Keyboard Modes (KEY 1-4)
- **12 chromatic pads** (C–B) send MIDI notes on `keyChannel` (ch2 while the link is free; the KEYMODE3 **KBOARD lock** — on by default — makes it follow the SEQ edit channel, see below)
- **ENC1** (KEYMODE): Octave –2..+8 (4 counts = 1 octave, clamped), **per track** — each of the 16 tracks keeps its own octave (default 3), so turning it here never moves another track's channel; a DRUM track defaults to 1 (see below)
- **ENC2** (KEYMODE): Scale select (54 scales, Chromatic = no filter)
- **DRUM track**: when the keyboard channel's track is a **DRUM** sequence the 12 pads are drum notes, not pitches — the **scale filter and the KEY/SCALE colors are bypassed** (no pad is ever dark or silent for being out of scale, and the KEYMODE2/KEYMODE4 chord engines stay out of it, so every pad plays its own chromatic note C–B). The row idles in **dim OrangeRed**, a pad whose note the playhead is sounding blinks **dim green**, and a voice muted in the ENCMODE drum-voice overlay reads **dim orange** (mute wins over the blink; touch still flashes white). The keyboard **octave snaps to 1** when it lands on a drum track, which puts C1–B1 (36–47) under the pads — the kit's own notes — and the octave knob still transposes from there. Octaves are **per track** here too: every track keeps its own octave (default 3, a DRUM row 1), and `keyOctTrackPoll()` swaps the setting in and out as the keyboard moves, so an octave turned on one track never moves another. Pressing a pad also **selects that drum voice** for step editing, the direct form of hold M1 + step N (C = part 1, C# = part 2 … D#2 = part 16): the voice under the finger is the voice the steps toggle
- **KEYMODE2**: Chord engine active
  - M1/M2/M3/NEXT = quality (Maj/Min/Dim/Sus) — momentary, beats **KeyLock**'s diatonic triad (root still snapped to the scale); **with KeyLock ON, a held quality button wins over the scale's triad for that press**
  - F1–F4 = extensions (6, m7, M7, 9) — toggle
  - **LEDs**: quality/extension pads idle in the KEY button's dim green (extensions slightly darker: less red/blue); touch lights them white
  - ENC3 = Root, **ENC4 = FScale (Force to scale)**, ENC5–7 = Bass/Spread/Density, ENC8 = Latch
    - **FScale ON**: every chord tone is snapped to the nearest pitch of the current scale before it is sent (ties flatten first), so no out-of-scale note ever reaches MIDI — duplicates collapse to one voice; Chromatic passes everything through; OFF = free voicings. The snap also applies **at playback**: with FScale ON the sequencer forces each stored chord step into the current scale as it is sent (stored notes stay raw), so **changing the scale recolors recorded progressions live**; OFF plays the recording exactly as stored
  - **PREV (hold)** = momentary encoder overlay (Root/Scale/Latch/KeyLock/Perf — `KLOCK` is **key lock**, the chord being the scale's diatonic triad until a held quality button overrides it; `PERF` is the **performance** switch, a two-state flag that stores ON/OFF and changes nothing else)

- **KEYMODE4** (double-click KEY): genre-driven chord proposals
  - The engine scores all 12 pitch classes for the slot **before the focus** (transition matrix × MOOD × MOTION × CADENCE × SURP, plus a **voice-leading term** that rewards common tones and stepwise motion and dents parallel fifths/octaves, **phrase/pair cadence terms** — ii–V and V–I at bar ends, the authentic/plagal landing in the pattern's last bar — and a **secondary-dominant class**: SURP-gated rolls turn a proposal into V/x (maj triad + m7, e.g. `II` = V/V) whose resolution target the next scoring pass pulls toward) and lays the whole scored degree set out on the keyboard: **hue-mapped by key as originally designed** (C = blue … B = red), **rank 0 = the next chord from the highest probability = hottest**, brightness stepping down the list so the colors tell how probable each degree is after the last chord; borrowed degrees open with **TENS ≥ 34** and light **orange** (closed ones stay dark); every knob is 0–100 (boot 50), 1 count = 1 unit. Every knob/genre turn re-ranks the proposals **dynamically**, repaints the keyboard live **and re-generates every unlocked step in place** — the progression playing in the sequencer follows the knobs in real time (locked steps are never touched; the first count of a tweak burst claims the undo slot, so M3-hold restores the pre-tweak progression)
  - The **header displays the progression and keeps it there**: `V vi > I IV` = the walk so far · `>` · the top-4 proposals — persistent (it re-ranks live with every knob/scale/root/focus change); transient messages such as the **scale mood** or write results (`I V vi IV +8`) flash over it, then the progression returns. The focused step needs no header marker — the sequencer pads already show it. The **first chord of the walk is the chord played in KEYMODE2** when you enter (its kept notes seed the chain), or the stored step before the focus after a focus move
  - **ENC row**: ENC1 = **GENRE** (18, clamps — the knob does not wrap), ENC2–8 = MOOD/TENS/MOTION/CAD/CPX/PHRASE/SURP (**MOOD** reads out as DARK/COOL/MIXED/WARM/LIGHT and **CAD** as the cadence pair `V-I`/`IV-I`/`V-vi`/`ii-V`; **PHRASE** reads by name and the rest as 0–100) — **MOOD** brightness bias, **TENS** tension (how wide the borrowed-degree window opens, and above 67 a doubled sus/alt weight), **MOTION** how hard the genre's transition matrix pulls the chain, **CAD** cadence, **CPX** complexity (how faithfully chord *qualities* follow the genre table — 0 = plain triads), **PHRASE** phrase template (FREE · AABB · ABAB · ABAC · AABA — the one knob that decides whether the pattern has an arc at all; details in the generation list below), **SURP** surprise (the chance a proposal rolls into a secondary dominant: 0 = never, 100 = 30 %); **PREV (hold)** = overlay with ENC1 Octave / ENC2 Scale (flashes the scale mood over the progression) / ENC3 Key / **ENC4 FScale** (force-to-scale ON/OFF — ON makes playback of recorded steps follow the scale, same switch as KEYMODE2) / **ENC5 SUB** / **ENC6 INV** / **ENC7 PAS** / **ENC8 COL** (the four POLY embellishment amounts — substitution probability, inversion bias, passing amount, colour amount — that a step's `EMB` target and M3/F1 write with), and **PREV (click)** = focus −1; **NEXT (tap)** = the sticky **EMB menu** (below) — while it is open the row becomes **ENC1 EMB / ENC2 PLACE / ENC3 ERA**, PREV hold still borrows it for Octave/Scale/Key/FScale and hands it back on release
  - **Press a proposed key** = audition the chord (unscored keys are silent) and **chain the progression**: the next proposals follow the chord you just heard, so repeated presses walk a sequence by ear — even in AUDITION with nothing written. The chain re-anchors on the stored steps whenever the focus moves; on release the header shows where the chain goes next. With **WRITE** on (boots in **AUDITION**) it also lands on the **focused step** and the focus auto-advances (**F4** tap = AUTO, boots off)
  - **Controls**: **NEXT tap** = the **EMB menu** (toggle, below) · **NEXT hold** = focus +1 · **M1** = WRITE ⇄ AUDITION (**dim red** = AUDITION, **bright red** = WRITE) · **M2** = the sticky **TONALITY menu** (below) · **M3 tap** = apply the generator to every unlocked step, **M3 hold** = undo (one-level, restores channel + attributes) · **F1** = VARIATION (reseed + generate) · **F2** = lock the focused step (apply skips it) · **F3** = VOICE-LEAD (inversions follow the bass line) · **F4 tap** = AUTO-ADVANCE (focus advances after every write, boots off) · **F4 held** = audition only (no write, no advance). After M3/F1/undo the header shows the **written progression** as numerals (`I V vi IV +8` = first four chords + how many follow)
  - **TONALITY page (M2 tap)** — a sticky encoder page in **four banks**, `F1`–`F4` pick one (the open bank's pad burns **white**, the other three drift through a **slow dim hue cycle**; the header's left slot names the bank). Every encoder stores its own value, `--` where a bank leaves a slot idle, and six slots **mirror** a control that lives elsewhere so page and PREV overlay can never disagree (`KEY` / `SCALE` / `FSCALE` bank 1, `SURP` bank 2, `VOICE-LEAD` bank 3, `PHRASE` bank 4). The defaults are neutral: opening the page and touching nothing sounds exactly as before.
    - **1 TONAL** (`TONAL`) — `KEY · SCALE · GRAV · MOD · MODTGT · MODLEN · FSCALE · CADSTR`: key and scale, **tonal gravity** (how hard tonic and dominant pull), the **modulation plan** (none / relative / parallel / to-degree) with its target degree and how many closing bars it covers (the tail is restated in the new key, or with the mode colour flipped for parallel), force-to-scale, and **cadence strictness** (scales every cadence term).
    - **2 CHROMA** — `BORROW · PREP · SD · MIX · DIAT · UNRES · SURP · STRICT`: a **hard borrow budget** per pattern (0 = unlimited), preparation (a chromatic chord with no common tone costs more), secondary-dominant density, modal mixture (borrowed chords from the parallel mode), diatonic bias, the unresolved penalty away from cadence points, Surp, and functional strictness (repeats at a bar end: dented, vetoed at 100).
    - **3 VOICE** — `PARAL · BASS · LEAP · DOUBL · CMNTN · TOP · LEAD · SPREAD`: parallel fifths (40 reproduces the old fixed dent, 100 vetoes), bass continuity, leap limit (50 leaves the voicing alone, above packs it tighter), doubling rule (root-doubled / free / strict), common-tone reward, top-voice motion, the voice-lead toggle, and register spread.
    - **4 ARC** — `RATE · ELIDE · CADWIN · CELL · VAR · ANTE · MOTIF · PHRASE`: harmonic rhythm (a new chord every N steps, held between), phrase overlap (elided bar ends skip their landing), cadence window, cell reuse, the variation operator `F1` uses (transpose / revoice / reorder / extend / substitute), antecedent relation (mirror / respond / parallel a fifth up), motif length, and Phrase.
    While the page is open `F1`–`F4` belong to the banks; VARIATION, LOCK, LEAD and AUTO come back the moment it closes. The page and its bank die with KEYMODE4.
  - **EMB menu (NEXT tap)** — what M3/F1 generate beyond the plain progression (the menu is KEYMODE4-only and closes when you leave the mode; locked steps are never touched):
    - **ENC1 EMB** (wraps): **OFF** = the plain generator as before · **PASS** = every odd step becomes a **passing chord** — a triad on the scale degree **one below the following main chord**, its quality/extension/inversion re-scored through the same genre+knob proposal logic (so it belongs to the grammar and voice-leads); when that degree is the previous main chord itself, the step simply keeps its main chord · **P+S** = PASS plus **substitution**: every main chord rolls once against the **SURP** knob and, if it hits, is swapped through a fixed table — **I→iii, ii→IV, iii→I, IV→ii, V→♭VII, vi→IV, ♭VII→V** (SURP 0 = never, 100 = always)
    - **ENC2 PLACE** (CW = **DIST**, CCW = **ODD**): **ODD** = every odd step passes when valid · **DIST** = only when the bracketing main chords stand **at least two scale degrees apart** — tighter neighbours keep their main chord (a near-touching "passing" chord would only wobble)
    - **ENC3 ERA** (clamps 0–100): the same genre weighting applied to *inversions* — 0 = root position. It moved here when **PHRASE** took the row slot; unlike `EMB` and `PLACE` a turn regenerates the unlocked steps straight away
    - **PHRASE** (encoder row `ENC7`, clamps): **FREE** · **AABB** · **ABAB** · **ABAC** · **AABA** — gives the chain a **goal**. The engine is causal (one weighted draw per step), so a generated pattern has no arc and no ending; a template plans one. The phrase-end cadence comes from the **CADENCE** knob, which selects in four bands: under 25 **HALF** (`ii–V`, nothing to resolve), under 50 **PLAGAL** (`IV–I`), under 75 **AUTHENTIC** (`V–I`, the boot default at 50), above **DECEPTIVE** (`V–vi`) — its continuous value still scales the cadence terms, so one knob both aims and weights. The degree that cadence lands on becomes the plan for the **last bar**, and every earlier bar end is walked **backward** from it through the genre table (the degree that leads best into the bar after it; the cadence approach is preferred one bar out, the tonic at the head). The forward pass then scores those landings **×8**, and the proposal ranking uses the same plan, so at a planned bar end what M3 writes is what the keyboard offered. **AABB / ABAB / ABAC / AABA** are a **bar-level letter map**: a bar whose letter has already played replays the first bar that carried it, so **AABB** is one cell for bars 1–2 plus a second, repeated cell for bars 3–4, **ABAB** alternates two cells, **AABA** returns to the opening cell for its last bar, and **ABAC** repeats only bar 3 from bar 1. A repeat is written through the generation tick, so the next new bar answers what was actually heard rather than the material it replaced; a bar's planned landing and any locked step always win, so the cadence arc survives. On a short pattern the map is read from its first bars: at 32 steps **AABB** and **AABA** repeat bar 1, while **ABAB** and **ABAC** keep two new cells. **FREE** is the plain chain. The arc comes from the bar-end plan either way
    - **Why it landed** (diagnostic): after M3/F1 the header names the plan and what decided the phrase end — `Plan V-I PLAN` (the backward pass changed the winner), `… CAD` (the grammar agreed and the cadence pair did the work), `… GENR` (the genre matrix alone); with **FREE** it reads `Cadence V-vi`, the cadence the knob has armed. Arc-level only: the individual scoring terms are not attributed
    - The header after M3 counts every written step including the passing ones (`… +16` for a full pattern)
  - **Step pads**: press = move the focus (**white** in AUDITION, **red** in WRITE; locked = amber); a **tap** disables the step only while **WRITE** is on (**AUDITION** keeps it — nothing is written there); a **hold** overlays the encoder row onto that step — **ENC1 OCT** (per-step playback octave −2…+2, stored chord untouched), **ENC2 DEG** (shift every tone one scale degree in the key, intervals keep their shape), **ENC3 INV** (cycle the bass up / top down an octave, refuses to land on an existing tone), **ENC4 VOICE** (spread/close the upper voices one octave around the bass). Release hands the row back (or to the open EMB menu); the header echoes each turn (`Step 5 Octave +1`, `Step 5 Degree vi`, `Step 5 Inversion 1st`, `Step 5 Voicing 2 oct`). A hold in **WRITE** still arms the usual assign gesture on top
  - **PLAY** works here too: start/stop the transport without leaving KEYMODE4
  - Nothing persists across a reboot (RAM only, by design); the progression grammar follows the current scale (7-note scales exactly, otherwise major degrees from the root)

  - **KEYMODE3**: the **channel-picker variant** — the step pads are the variant-3 **channel overlay** (yellow): a pad press sets the keyboard output channel. **ENC1 = OCT**, **ENC2 = SCALE** (same as every variant), and **ENC3 = ENCOD** / **ENC4 = KBOARD** are the **channel-link switches** (two-state `free`/`locked`, **no wrap** — CW saturates at `locked`, CCW at `free`, a turn never flips past the end; header echoes each change):
  - **free**: each mode keeps its own channel exactly as always — the ENCMODE3 encoder CCs, the KEYMODE3 keyboard and the SEQMODE3 edit focus change independently through their own overlays
  - **locked** (boot default): that family's channel is **linked to the SEQ edit channel** (the hub). Any channel picked through any of the three variant-3 overlays then moves **all locked families at once** — e.g. with both locked, a pad on the keyboard's overlay sets the keyboard channel, the SEQ edit channel and the encoder channel together; same from the ENCMODE3 or SEQMODE3 overlay. Locked families boot onto the hub channel. The flags persist in the SEQ EEPROM block; a save written before the default flipped carries no explicit choice, so it too boots locked — the stored byte only counts once the knob has been turned
